## Game Data

The hitzone data under `docs/data/` is generated by
[scripts/build-data.mjs](scripts/build-data.mjs) from tables decoded by
[scripts/extract-hitzones.mjs](scripts/extract-hitzones.mjs) out of
Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate's own RomFS `dt_tune` files, dumped by
the author from a personally-owned cartridge. No game files are
redistributed. The extraction was made possible by
[Atmosphère](https://github.com/Atmosphere-NX/Atmosphere),
[hekate](https://github.com/CTCaer/hekate), Lockpick_RCM and
[nxdumptool](https://github.com/DarkMatterCore/nxdumptool), and by the
[Ryujinx](https://github.com/Ryujinx/Ryujinx) emulator's RomFS extractor.

### gatheringhallstudios / JoeLago community database

English monster names, and the body-part names shown where a monster's
extracted table byte-matches the community part list, derive from the MHGU
database bundled in [JoeLago/MHGUDB-iOS](https://github.com/JoeLago/MHGUDB-iOS)
(MIT-licensed), built on the community database from
[gatheringhallstudios/MHGenDatabase](https://github.com/gatheringhallstudios/MHGenDatabase).
Only factual data is extracted (`data-src/db/`); no source code, schema, or
image assets from those projects are redistributed.

### Kiranico (https://mhgu.kiranico.com/)

Kiranico's MHGU database was used to verify the decoded hitzone tables, and
as the independent second opinion that gates which monsters get body-part
names (see "Part names" in the README). All damage values in this app come
from the game files, not from Kiranico, and no Kiranico text is
redistributed — only the decision of whether their part list agreed with
MHGUDB's. This attribution is offered as courtesy acknowledgment of their
fan-database work.
