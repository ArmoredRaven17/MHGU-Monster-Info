## Game Data

The hitzone data under `docs/data/` is generated by
[scripts/build-data.mjs](scripts/build-data.mjs) from tables decoded from
Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate's own RomFS `dt_tune` files, dumped by
the author from a personally-owned cartridge. No game files are
redistributed. The extraction was made possible by
[Atmosphère](https://github.com/Atmosphere-NX/Atmosphere),
[hekate](https://github.com/CTCaer/hekate), Lockpick_RCM and
[nxdumptool](https://github.com/DarkMatterCore/nxdumptool), and by the
[Ryujinx](https://github.com/Ryujinx/Ryujinx) emulator's RomFS extractor.
